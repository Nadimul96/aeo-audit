How Long Does Cold Brew Last?

By Maya Okafor, head roaster. Published April 2, 2026; last reviewed September 18, 2026.

Cold brew is forgiving, but not immortal. In our 2025 shelf-life log of 40 batches, concentrate held its flavor for about two weeks and diluted drinks faded within four days. The sections below answer the storage questions we hear most, with the temperatures and times we actually tested.

How long does cold brew last in the fridge?

Sealed cold brew concentrate keeps 10 to 14 days at 38°F (3°C) or below, based on our 2025 log. After day 14, 6 of 40 batches tasted noticeably flat. Store it in a glass jar with a tight lid and keep it on a shelf rather than the door, where the temperature swings by several degrees.

Does diluting cold brew shorten its life?

Yes. Once you add water or milk, plan to finish the drink within 3 to 4 days. Dilution lowers the acidity that slows spoilage, and milk adds its own clock: dairy-based cold brew should be treated like milk and used by its date.

Can you freeze cold brew?

You can. Frozen in ice-cube trays, cold brew keeps its flavor for roughly 3 months, and the cubes are useful for iced coffee that does not water down. Thaw in the fridge, not on the counter, and use within 2 days.

How can you tell cold brew has gone bad?

Trust your nose first. A sour, musty or fermented smell, cloudiness that was not there before, or any film on the surface means it should be discarded. Flat taste alone is not a safety problem, but it is a sign the batch is past its best.

Should cold brew be left out at room temperature?

No. Food-safety guidance from the FDA says perishable drinks should not sit out more than 2 hours, or 1 hour above 90°F. Cold brew is low in acid compared with hot coffee, so the same rule applies.
